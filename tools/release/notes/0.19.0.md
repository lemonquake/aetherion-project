# Aetherion Project 0.19.0
- Malasugue Mall has been rebuilt with different wall finishes, stone borders, patterned walkways, repaired floors, damaged shops and more signs of the outbreak.
- Enter two damaged elevators in the Level 1 north lobby. Torn cabin roofs reveal the shafts, cables and machinery overhead. Both lifts have failing white lights, blood trails, abandoned stretchers and debris.
- Find six new cover and hiding spots on the upper floors, with views across the atrium. There is also a service recess between the elevators.
- Working lights make nearby paths and rooms easier to see, while unlit corners stay dark.
- Footsteps now match tile, rubber and escalator treads. Standing still on a moving escalator no longer plays walking sounds.
- Firing a weapon briefly lights nearby walls and floors. Reduce flashes remains available in Settings.
- Fixed furniture disappearing when jumping on the top gallery.
- Fixed flickering floor sections and unstable floor textures.
- Launcher 1.2.0 has new mall artwork. Update the game to 0.19.0 to join the servers.
