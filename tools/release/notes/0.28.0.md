# Aetherion Project 0.28.0
- Deathmatch: frag grenades now lie on the map. Every round, on every Deathmatch map, four grenades are placed at random in corners and inside buildings, always somewhere you can walk to and never at a team's spawn. Walk up and press E to take one, then hold G to cook it and let go to throw.
- Those four are the only grenades in a Deathmatch round: kits, floor loot and boxes carry none, and the next round clears them and places four new ones.
- Bots that see a grenade go for it about one time in three. A bot carrying one always throws it at the enemy: cooked, on a clear arc, at someone it can see or a teammate just called out, and never where its own team stands.
- Update the game to 0.28.0 on every device to join the updated servers.
