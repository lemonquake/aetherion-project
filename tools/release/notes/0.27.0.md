# Aetherion Project 0.27.0
- New Deathmatch map: Hidden Forest. An ancient city swallowed by the jungle, always at night, for three teams of up to seven. Fight over the Temple of the Sun, the Heart of the Forest pyramid with a crypt through its base, the Moon Well, the Ball Court, the Observatory and the Court of Skulls.
- The jungle floor rises and falls: trails and clearings run between steep earth banks, and temple tops, terraces, the ball court platforms and a rope bridge make a second level above them.
- Torches and braziers burn with live, flickering flames, vines hang from the ruins and fireflies drift over every clearing. Narrow hidden paths cut between the areas, two of them under giant fig roots.
- Bots know Hidden Forest from the first match: its routes, stairs, hiding places and sniping spots.
- The official Deathmatch server takes Hidden Forest in turn with the other maps.
- Update the game to 0.27.0 on every device to join the updated servers.
