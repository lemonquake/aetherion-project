# Aetherion Project 0.22.0
- Deathmatch rounds end again: bots no longer camp in one spot forever. A bot never takes the spot it just held, or one close to it, for three minutes.
- When a side is down to two, or nobody has met an enemy for a while, bots cut their holds short and sweep the whole map with their team, checking the places nobody has looked at for longest. After a long quiet they get a rough idea of where the nearest enemy is, so a last player hiding is always found.
- Bot difficulty: Easy, Normal, Hard or Pro, set by the host in the lobby or in Host a game. Normal is the default. Easy bots react slowly and miss most shots. Hard bots aim better, react faster and roam more. Pro bots fight by their gun: they keep their distance behind a scope with a sniper rifle, rush you when down to a pistol, and push when you reload, are hurt, look away or are outnumbered.
- The bots' thinking has been rebuilt on state machines and behaviour trees, so every decision has a clear place and priority.
- Weapon attachments in the Deathmatch loadout: in Game Setup, choose a sight, muzzle, grip, magazine, stock and light for your primary and secondary. Each gun remembers its own setup, and an extended magazine holds more rounds. On phones they have their own tab.
- Fixed the heavy lag on Freight Delight when many players and bots fire at once (it could drop below 10 frames a second). The game was spending most of each frame on rounds that had already missed and flown far past the map; big firefights now keep their frame rate on every map, and the official servers keep up too.
- Muzzle flashes far from you no longer cast shadows, which saves a lot of drawing in a firefight. Your own flash and nearby ones still do.
- When anyone throws a grenade, everyone within 38 m hears a radio-distorted "Fire in the hole!".
- Freight Delight has 40 more lights in its darkest lanes, the Container Canyon and the edges of the alleys.
- The AWP now shows its picture in Game Setup.
- The Windows launcher shows each official server's bot difficulty.
- Update the game to 0.22.0 on every device to join the updated servers.
