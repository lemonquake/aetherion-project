# Aetherion Project 0.17.0
- New Deathmatch map: Malasugue Mall, the morning after the outbreak swept through it. Five storeys, three atriums, 114 shops, a cinema and a food hall. Pick it in the lobby under Map.
- The power is mostly out: pools of light under the downlights that still work, whole stretches gone dark, lights flickering and buzzing, emergency lamps running down. Bring a flashlight.
- Wreckage everywhere: smashed shopfronts, ceilings come down with sparking cables, overturned benches and carts, litter, blood, barricades, a burnt-out shop, a triage post, and a giant screen fallen on the holiday stage.
- Alex Jackie, a three-floor department store, joined inside by escalators that stopped when the power went (climb them like stairs).
- Real restrooms on every level, and the big public ones on Level 3.
- The mall's own escalators still move and carry you, up or down. Fire stairs in every corner.
- Shopfronts, glass doors and glass railings shatter when shot or blasted.
- On official servers the first player in the lobby is its host: change the settings and press Start. Others press Ready, and the host can start once everyone is Ready. Alone, you start at once against bots.
- Phones: the move stick reaches full running speed from about two-thirds of its ring, and pushing it to the edge sprints.
- Classic: the drop from the plane can no longer hurt you, even a hard landing.
- Faster loading on the official servers, and slightly faster map building everywhere.
