# Aetherion Project 0.24.0
- New Workbench for attachments: press K in a match (the Gear button on phones, or Workbench in Game Setup). Your gun turns on a stand in 3D; drag an attachment onto it and it snaps into place. Hover one to see exactly what it does, and the gun's numbers show the change before you fit it. Drag it back or press × to take it off.
- 11 new attachments: rifle and pistol/SMG drum magazines (twice the rounds, slower to reload), lightweight magazines, a marksman suppressor, a tactical compensator, stubby and heavy foregrips, a green laser, rail covers and a quick-deploy sling. A drum goes into the gun in place of its magazine and comes out with your hand when you reload.
- Scopes show a clear picture again. The 4x Thermal Scope no longer turns everything grey and white: it marks enemies with warm brackets, even through smoke and darkness. Night vision brightens the dark in natural colour instead of green.
- Hold ~ for the scoreboard: kills, deaths and assists, damage dealt and taken, headshot and grenade kills, and deaths from each. The end-of-game screen shows the same board.
- Deathmatch: a big banner announces the team that won the round, with a 3-second countdown to the next one. Each team's alive count shows under its name. The lag spike when a new round starts is gone.
- Spectating: dying while aiming through a scope no longer leaves the camera zoomed in. Press E to switch views (behind, over the shoulder, overhead, and a free camera when you play alone against bots), and R to watch everyone instead of only your team.
- The pause menu no longer stays on screen after you leave a match.
- Bots now follow waypoints on every map, along the middle of every corridor, street, bridge, doorway, stair and escalator, so they no longer walk into walls, shelves or pillars. They prefer main roads, avoid stretches where teammates got stuck, open doors in their way and go round locked ones.
- Sitio Kalinaw: bots can cross the river and the estero now. Its bridges were missing from the bots' map, so half the town was out of their reach.
- Update the game to 0.24.0 on every device to join the updated servers.
