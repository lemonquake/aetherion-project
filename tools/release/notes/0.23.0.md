# Aetherion Project 0.23.0
- Bots now follow waypoints on every map: thousands of points along the middle of every corridor, lane, street, bridge, doorway, stair and escalator, joined only where a body has been checked to fit with room to spare. A bot keeps to the line between two waypoints, so it no longer walks into walls, shelves or pillars, and if it is pushed off the line it plans its way back.
- Every waypoint has a name and a priority: main roads, side ways and dead ends. Bots prefer the main roads, the way players do, and the places they hold (hiding, sniping and cover spots) are waypoints too.
- When bots get stuck somewhere, every bot on the team avoids that stretch for a while, and the game remembers it for the next match.
- Sitio Kalinaw: bots can cross the river and the estero now. Its bridges, planks and the culvert were missing from the bots' map, so half the town was out of their reach.
- Bots open doors in their way, and go round a locked one.
- Bots sent somewhere they cannot quite reach (a ping on a wall, a teammate on a roof) stop at the nearest place instead of standing there trying.
- Malasugue Mall can be edited in the Godot editor: its waypoints, and anything added to the mall (walls, crates, cover). See docs/WAYPOINTS.md.
- Update the game to 0.23.0 on every device to join the updated servers.
