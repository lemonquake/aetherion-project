# Aetherion Project 0.21.0
- New Deathmatch map: Freight Delight, a squatter settlement grown inside an old container yard by the port. Two teams of up to 12, and it is always night.
- Three ways across: the Container Canyon, the Bodega to the crane plaza to the Talyer, and the Eskinita shanty alleys. Above them is a second floor of container decks, plaza bridges, the crane platform and the Taas, reached by three stairs.
- The yard is lit by its own floodlights, bare bulbs, flickering tubes, videoke neon and shrine candles. Its field is disabled by default, as in the mall.
- Deathmatch maps are now listed as Malasugue Mall, Freight Delight, Lawless Sub. Malasugue Mall is the default, and the official Deathmatch server plays them in that order.
- Bots know Freight Delight's routes and vantage points on both floors.
- Bots no longer get stuck in walls, above all in Malasugue Mall. Every map's walkways are now checked against the real walls, counters, kiosks and wreckage, so paths no longer run through a wall or into a sealed shop. Bots use the middle of doorways and corridors and steer off pillars and corners.
- Bots learn each map: waypoints, hiding spots and sniping spots on every floor, worked out once and saved. In the mall that includes the back of each shop, the top of each escalator and the atrium balconies.
- Bots learn from play: spots that are held often, or that get kills, become popular. A spot where you camp or score from becomes one they use too. What they learn is saved for the next match.
- In Deathmatch, looters now lie in ambush at hiding spots. Bots no longer stand facing a wall while they hold a spot or wait beside their squad leader.
- The Windows launcher shows which map each official server is playing.
- Update the game to 0.21.0 on every device to join the updated servers.
