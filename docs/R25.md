# r25 (0.25.0): Malasugue Mall firefight lag, sprint carry, firing breaks a sprint, muzzle smoke, stair speed

Requested 2026-11-01 (@lemondev). Branch `lemonquake/malasugue-mall-perf-ff2e6a` (off the 0.24.0 release, 65d20b4).
Evidence in `work/r25/`.

## What changed

| Request | Cause found | Change | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Massive lag in Malasugue Mall when many fire | Measured with `--autotest=firefight` on the mall (10 guns, RTX 4060): 11 ms quiet -> 16-18 ms firing, CPU-bound. (1) Every shattered pane spawned 14-44 shard rigid bodies, each with its own BoxMesh and casting shadows; past 140 they were frozen but **never freed**, so a match of mall glass left thousands of bodies drawn every frame. (2) All 72 sound voices busy while firing; every sound started a ~0.5 ms path search through the acoustic portals (bullet impacts never hit the 1.5 m cache). (3) The first round into each breakable prop model cut its mesh into chunks on the spot (7.8 ms hitch). (4) Impact dust and haze emitted for hits nobody could see. | 8-18 shards a pane, one shared unit box, no shadows from small debris, 70 tumbling + 160 resting kept (oldest freed); a sound plays at most 3 times at once (6 for loud ones; a shot takes over its oldest copy, a quiet sound the farthest one if nearer), no path search when no voice is free, 4 fresh path searches a frame (8 for loud sounds) and the zone's last path or a rough one beyond; 3 impact sounds a frame; impact particles only inside the view frustum; breakable models cut during map load (145 ms on the mall) | `fx/effects.gd`, `audio/sound_system.gd`, `world/dynamics.gd`, `props/physics_prop.gd` |
| Weapon disappears when sprinting | Sprinting set the viewmodel's "lowered" pose (the one for the SmarTab), which hides the gun at 98 % | A separate sprint carry: low, canted across the chest, still drawn; snaps up in 0.1 s | `player/viewmodel.gd` (`_sprint`) |
| Shooting at a sprint should drop to normal speed | Nothing stopped a sprint while the trigger was held | The trigger held (and 0.35 s after) blocks the sprint in the shared Motor (server, prediction and bots alike; `sprint_lock` is in the prediction state) | `player/motor.gd`, `player/character.gd` |
| Less muzzle smoke | 5 cards a shot, alpha 0.28, 1.3 s, growing 3.5x, lit by the 13-energy flash right at the muzzle: a glowing orange cloud over the sights in automatic fire | 3 cards, alpha 0.14, 0.8 s, grow 2.4; at most one puff per 0.25 s per gun; the shooter's own puff half size, every 0.6 s at most, 0.8 m out past the flash | `fx/particles.gd`, `fx/effects.gd` |
| Speed boost up the mall stairs | The fire stairs are smooth 32.5 degree ramps. Running up one at an angle (sliding along its wall) the step-up code took the ramp rising ahead for a kerb and shoved the body 8 cm on, every tick: 8-9.5 m/s at a run (run is 4.4) | A step must rise above the slope underfoot (`_slope_rise`), so the ramp itself is never one; kerbs and real steps are unchanged | `player/character.gd` `_try_step` |

## Measurements (RTX 4060, 1280x720)

- Stairs, `--autotest=bursts` on the mall (new `mall` part: every first-storey fire flight straight on and at +-32
  degrees from the landing, the department store stairs, the up escalators, run and sprint): before 32 of 58 runs
  burst (worst tick 11.9 m/s, worst 0.1 s 8.4 m/s), after 0 of 58 (worst 6.3 = sprint). `work/r25/stairs_diag`
  (before), `work/r25/reg_bursts` (after, the test as committed).
- Glass aftermath, `--autotest=firefight --ff_glass=150` (150 panes nearest the camera shattered, then measured;
  `--ff_legacy_debris` for the old pieces): legacy 2790 pieces kept, 1938 draws, 14.5 ms; r25 230 pieces, 1230 draws,
  12.7 ms. `work/r25/glass_legacy`, `work/r25/glass_r25`.
- Firing phases of the same benchmark (a minute, before most glass is broken): 16.1 ms before, 17.1 ms after, within
  this benchmark's run-to-run noise. Playing voices 72 -> 44-51, shard pieces capped at 230. On this machine the
  short benchmark does not show the gain; the accumulation above does. Not measured on a low-end PC or a real
  multi-player server.
- `--autotest=r25` (`work/r25/r25b`): gun drawn and on screen at a sprint; trigger pulled at a sprint -> sprint off after
  1 tick, 4.40 m/s while firing, back to 6.19 m/s after letting go; 15 rounds of automatic fire -> 2 own smoke puffs.

## Tests

- `bash work/r25/run.sh <name> r25 [port]`: `--autotest=r25` on a Malasugue Mall Deathmatch host (`--r25_only=sprint,fire,smoke`).
- `bash work/r25/run.sh <name> bursts [port]`: stair and escalator speeds on the mall.
- `bash work/r25/ff.sh <name> [port] [--ff_glass=150] [--ff_legacy_debris]`: the mall firefight benchmark from the
  Grand Atrium (prints voices and debris per phase).
- `bash work/r25/reg.sh <name> <port> <args...>`: any other autotest (regressions: `--autotest=bursts`, `canal`, `mall`).

Gotchas: `tools/fix_infer.py` (run by `tools/check.sh`) resolves paths against the main checkout, so in a worktree its
auto-fixes miss (fix inference errors by hand); `sed -i` in Git Bash turns CRLF files to LF (restore before committing);
the `builds/` folder here is a junction to the r24 worktree's (the release chunk store).
