# r24 (0.24.0): workbench, scopes, spectating, Deathmatch rounds, scoreboard

Requested 2026-11-01 (@lemondev). Branch `lemonquake/r24-attachments` (off r23). Evidence in `work/r24/`.

## What changed

| Request | Change | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Pause menu stays up after leaving a match | The pause menu lives under `Game.ui`; the controller now frees it (and the workbench) when it leaves the tree | `player_controller.gd` `_exit_tree` |
| 4x scope "white filter", scope filter effects | The white one was the **4x Thermal Scope** (lens turned flat grey, people as white blobs); night vision was green phosphor with grain. Now every scope shows the real picture: thermal outlines enemies with warm corner brackets (still through smoke/dark, never walls), night vision brightens the dark in natural colour (gain 1 in daylight = unchanged). Thinner dark rim inside every lens | `ui/scope_overlay.gd` |
| Spectator camera broken after dying scoped | The death and spectator cameras kept the scope's zoomed field of view; reset to the normal FOV | `player_controller.gd` `_process` |
| More spectator options | **E** cycles views: Behind, Over the shoulder, Overhead, Free camera (solo host only). **R** switches between teammates and everyone (only when no other human is in the match). Touch: Camera / Everyone buttons | `player_controller.gd`, `hud.gd` |
| DM round-win lag spike | The restart respawned all 21 players in one tick (79 ms) and then drew them all at once. Respawns now go out over several frames (2.5 ms budget per rendered frame, humans first; the round cannot end while players are still coming back). Worst frame 104 ms -> 37 ms | `match_server.gd` `_spawn_some` |
| Announce the round winner, restart 3 s later with a timer | Big "<Team> wins the round" banner in the team colour with "Next round in 3/2/1"; `round_break_s` 4 -> 3 | `hud.gd` `_round_banner_show`, `rules.json` |
| Alive players per team (DM) | "N alive" / "Out" under every team name in the top bar, always | `hud.gd` team bar |
| `~` scoreboard, also after the game | Hold `~` (QuoteLeft, rebindable): per team, K / D / A, damage dealt, damage received, headshot kills, deaths from headshots, grenade kills, deaths from grenades. The results screen shows the same board | `ui/scoreboard.gd`, `match_server.gd` (`on_damage`, `_score_death`, `board`), `world.gd` (`request_board` RPC), `results_ui.gd` |
| New way to add attachments | **Workbench** (K in a match, phone "Gear" button, "Workbench" in Game Setup): the gun as a 3D model on a turntable (drag to turn, wheel to zoom), mounts on the left, fitting attachments on the right. Drag one onto the gun (or its mount): it flies to its mount, snaps on with a green "+" burst; hover shows exactly what it does ("+20% weapon sway resistance", "+30 rounds per magazine (30 -> 60)"), and the stats panel previews the change. × or dragging back takes it off | `ui/workbench_ui.gd`, `weapons/attachments.gd` (`effects`), `weapons/item_models.gd` |
| More magazines, muzzles, grips, extras | 11 new: Rifle Drum, Pistol/SMG Drum, Lightweight Rifle / Pistol-SMG magazines, Marksman Suppressor, Tactical Compensator, Stubby Grip, Heavy Foregrip, Green Laser Module, Ergonomic Rail Covers, Quick-Deploy Sling. Models made in Blender by code; every attachment has a one-line description | `tools/r24_attachments.py`, `tools/blender/make_loot_models.py`, `weapons.json` (`cap_mult`, `desc`), `loot.json` |

Also fixed in passing: recycling a bullet-hole decal whose door or window had been destroyed assigned a freed object to a
typed variable (log spam every hit; `fx/effects.gd` `_decal`).

## Tests

`bash work/r24/run_r24.sh <name> [port] [--r24_only=workbench,board,scopes,round,spectate,pause] [--map=lawless_sub]`
(Deathmatch listen host, 20 bots; use `--profile=r24test` so Game Setup's bench does not touch your profile).

- workbench: K opens it, hover card text, a real mouse drag of the drum onto the gun (60 rounds), every slot fitted
  through the drop handler (the replaced sight goes back to the bag), turn/zoom by mouse, × removes to the bag, Esc
  closes, Game Setup's bench fits into the profile.
- board: headshot / grenade kill / assist counted; ~ shows and hides the board; alive counts match the server.
- scopes: every magnified sight aimed at an enemy in the open: no grey-out, no green, not washed out (by day and by
  night with `--map=lawless_sub --r24_time=100`).
- round: frame times from the last kill through the next round; banner text; 3 s break.
- spectate: FOV after dying scoped; four views; free camera flies; watching an enemy alone vs bots.
- pause (last): the pause menu open when leaving the match is gone from the main menu.

## Not done / known

- The drum hangs under the gun's own magazine (as the extended magazines do) instead of replacing it.
- The workbench in a match offers what is in your bag and on your other guns, not what lies on the ground nearby.
- The commercial A/B benchmark gate of the Gauntlet Loop was not run (no reference captures on disk).
