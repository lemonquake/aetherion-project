# r16 (0.17.0): Malasugue Mall after the outbreak

User request (2026-10-30, @lemondev): far more detail in the Mall (it looked empty and sterile); less bright, with
assets that fit the lighting; dark spots, flickering and faulty lights; a huge department store with dark spots of
its own; zombie-apocalypse destruction and mess, blood stains, drags and splatters; real restrooms on Level 3. Then:
build the game and update the launcher and the server.

## What changed

- **Lighting** (`mall_layout.indoor_light`, `lighting.gd`, new `mall_lights.gd`): the uniform warm fill (1.05) and
  bright ambient (0.52) are now a faint cold fill (0.16) and ambient (0.12). Light comes from fittings that still
  have power (`MallBuilder.power`: value noise gives blacked-out stretches tens of metres across, lit stretches and a
  patchwork between): 220 downward spot lights on the walks, 57 faulty ones (flicker, starter, dying, brownout;
  Lighting animates them and draws their lens), shop lights by shop (a third dark, a fifth with one faulty fitting,
  long anchors by stretch), 57 battery emergency lamps with exit signs in the dark stretches (some running down),
  daylight shafts through the East Court dome and West Court vault (shadowed, dimmer at night), four narrow beams in
  the Grand Atrium, a dusty volumetric haze and dark depth fog, a smoky overcast sky with the city burning on the
  horizon. Dead fittings, ceiling strips, court light lines, icicles, tree lights and signs (`mall_signs_dead`) are
  drawn unlit. Faulty fittings near the player hum and crackle in time with their flicker (`lamp_buzz`, generated by
  `tools/make_r16_sounds.py`).
- **Dirt** (`surface.gdshader` floor_patch, `mall_vc.gdshader`, `materials.gd`): patchy dirt tracked over every
  floor, grime at the foot of walls and painted fittings.
- **Wreckage** (new `mall_wreck.gd`, decals from new `tools/make_mall_apoc.py` -> `assets/mall/apoc.png`, plus the
  blood and vandal atlases): per level ~70 floor decals (papers, dirt, oil, dust), carts (some on their sides),
  dropped luggage, bags, shoes and phones, rubbish bags, cans and bottles; blood stories (pools with bodies dragged
  to doors, bloody footprints, spatter, handprints); four ceiling collapses (holes, hanging tiles, cables, a dangling
  downlight, debris and dust; one sparking with a flashing light), a leak with a puddle; survivors' spray paint,
  blood writing, taped notices and bullet holes on cores, columns and restroom fronts. Set pieces: the Grand
  Atrium's south LED screen fallen on the holiday stage (cables, sparks, a body under it, police tape; the other
  screen glitching), a triage post (cots, drip stands, screens, body bags, quarantine board), barricades (Level 3
  restrooms, the cinema lobby on Level 5), a burnt-out shop on Level 4 (soot, scorch, rubble, embers, smoke and a fog
  volume), a failed checkpoint outside (burnt and flipped police cars, a crashed van, barriers, sandbags, tape).
  Shops: 112 panes smashed (glass on the floor), 29 shutters down, stock and papers on the floors, blood in a third.
  Decor: benches tipped, planters knocked over, bins kicked over, kiosks looted, banners torn down, food-hall chairs
  overturned, the tree's top strings dead.
- **Alex Jackie department store** (new `mall_dept.gd`; layout `DEPT_ESC`, `dept_well`): the east anchor on Levels
  1-3 (91 m x 14.5 m a floor), joined inside by two stopped escalators (real steps, a ramp to walk, the openings in
  the floors lined and railed, "Escalator not running" signs). Departments under hanging signs: cosmetics and
  fragrance counters (some smashed), bags, shoes, customer service; kids, clearance sale, ladies, fitting rooms, men
  and a stock room behind a staff door ("DEAD INSIDE DO NOT OPEN", a body bag); home and living room sets, bedding,
  toys, appliances. Power only in parts of each floor.
- **Restrooms** (new `mall_restrooms.gd`): every block rebuilt with tiled walls, vanities (basins, taps, soap,
  mirrors), hand dryers, cubicles with partitions and doors (some open, some off a hinge), toilets with cisterns,
  urinals with screens, bins, signs, a flickering tube; and on Level 3 the big public restrooms in the unit beside the
  north-west block (gents: five urinals, four cubicles; ladies: seven cubicles, baby-changing shelf; a shut cubicle
  wedged with a broom, "HUWAG BUKSAN", blood under the door; "MAMA I'M OK" on the mirror; bedding, cans and a radio
  where people sheltered), with the barricade outside and signs standing out over the walk.
- **Fix**: the department store helper and the shop builder referenced each other, which kept the whole map builder
  alive after every build (a leak each round); broken, and the exit leak report matches r15 again.

## State

- Lighting, wreckage, store, restrooms: **IMPLEMENTED - benchmark UNVERIFIED** (no commercial reference image on this
  machine; the blind review compares r15 with r16). Mechanics checks above pass; phones not measured.
- Bots and the store: **FAIL** (`mall_dept_bot`). Bots are not linked over the stopped escalators (one stalled beside
  the wedge); a bot sent from inside the store on Level 1 to its Level 3 stalled at the store's south end by the
  stair core (65, 0, 21 then 57, 0, 40). Bots elsewhere route as before (L1 -> L5 in 29 s).

## Open gaps

- Blood readability (the critic's gap): pools and drags read black or brown in the dark; recheck: a recognisably dark-red pool, smear or drag in the food hall view and two others, frame as readable as now.
- `mall_dept_bot`: bot paths out of the east anchor's south end; bot links over the stopped escalators.
- Frame-time tail on Level 5 (p99 24.7 ms vs 18.6 in r15); phones (8 lights per mesh on the Mobile renderer, no
  volumetric fog) not measured.
- The big restroom's standing-out sign crosses its front sign; decals on glass were left out (they would float
  after the glass breaks).
