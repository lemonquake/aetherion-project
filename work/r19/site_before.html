Deathmatch

Lawless Sub · Malasugue Mall

Up to three teams of 7 on two maps: the subdivision, or a five-storey mall with working escalators and glass that shatters, now the night after the outbreak: dark the whole match, wrecked and bloodied, with each team starting on its own floor. Choose the map on the Home screen; the official server alternates them. Pick a primary and a secondary gun; everyone starts with armour and a helmet. Win on team score or on the clock (the fallen respawn), or as the last team standing.

Get the game

Your game must be the same version as the servers: 0.18.0.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

  1. Download AetherionLauncher.exe and open it.
  2. If Windows says Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. The launcher is not code-signed yet.
  3. The launcher installs itself for your user (no admin needed) and adds a Start menu and desktop shortcut. Press Install to download the game: 1.11 GB download, 1.82 GB on disk.
  4. Press Play. Later updates download only what changed.
Download the launcher630 KB · version 1.1.0

Already installed? Open Aetherion Project from the Start menu. If it shows Update, press it, then Play.

Crashing on a laptop with Intel graphics? In the game open Settings > Graphics > Renderer and choose Safe graphics, then restart. The game also switches by itself after a crash on such laptops. Updating the Intel graphics driver helps too.

Android

Android 7.0 or newer, 64-bit · about 1.2 GB free space

  1. On your phone, download the APK. It is 569 MB, so use Wi-Fi.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  4. For a new version, download the APK again and install it over the old one. Your profile stays.
Download the APK569 MB · version 0.18.0

Black screen or a crash when the game starts? Install the compatibility build (569 MB) instead. It draws with OpenGL, which older phones handle better.

Mac, iPhone and Linux are not in this playtest yet. Play on a Windows PC or an Android phone for now.

How to playtest

Play together: agree on a time with the other testers so you land in the same match.

  1. 1

    Set your name

    Open Profile in the main menu and type the name other players will see.

  2. 2

    Choose a mode

    On Home, pick Classic (battle royale) or Deathmatch.

  3. 3

    Tap Play online

    Under Official server · Singapore, tap Play online. You join that mode's server in one tap.

  4. 4

    Start or ready up

    If you are the first one in, you are the host: pick the map and settings, then press Start match. Otherwise tap Ready; the host starts once everyone is ready. If a match is already running, you watch it and play the next one.

Play now on the Home screen is offline practice against bots. Use it to learn the controls while you wait for other testers.

What's new in 0.18.0

  • Deathmatch: the host picks how the round is won. Score limit (first team to 10, 20, 30 or 50 kills), Timer (10, 14 or 30 minutes; the best team score wins), or Last team standing. With a score limit or timer, fallen players respawn at their team's spawn after 5 seconds, with a few seconds of spawn protection.
  • The forcefield now squeezes anyone it pins against a wall or object: heavy damage that grows until it kills, in about two seconds. A crowd pressed against a wall no longer protects the people in it.
  • Every match on every map starts at night. The host chooses how long each night and each day lasts: 2, 5 or 10 minutes.
  • Malasugue Mall stays dark the whole match. No sunrise indoors.
  • Malasugue Mall: the three teams start on different floors (Level 1, 3 and 5), shuffled every match.
  • The win rule and the night and day length can be set on the Home screen and in Host a game, as well as in the lobby.
  • Sidewalks and raised ground: stepping off a kerb or a sidewalk edge no longer shakes you in place until you jump (60 of 80 test crossings failed before, about 3 now, all against real obstacles), and stepping up a kerb keeps your running speed.
  • Vaulting: anything you can see over, you can vault. Malasugue Mall's balustrades (0 of 144 vaultable before, 144 of 144 now), upper-floor windows (0 of 40 before, all now; you drop down the other side, with fall damage), low French-window sills, and railings with posts. Bots won't vault into a drop of more than about 3 m.
  • Sitio Kalinaw: house stairs are wider (up to 0.92 m, from 0.78 m), with room to climb through the floor above and step off at the top.
  • Fix for crashes on laptops with Intel graphics and 4 GB of memory: a new Safe graphics mode (OpenGL). The game switches to it by itself after a crash on such laptops, and you can choose it in Settings > Graphics > Renderer. Low-memory PCs also start on the Low preset.
  • Launcher 1.1.0: says when the game closed unexpectedly and stays out of the way while the game restarts itself.
