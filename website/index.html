Deathmatch

Malasugue Mall · Freight Delight · Lawless Sub

Three maps. Malasugue Mall is a five-storey mall with damaged shops, two bloodied elevators and hiding spots along the upper galleries, and each team starts on its own floor. Freight Delight is a container-yard slum at night for two teams of up to 12, with a second floor reached by three stairs. Lawless Sub is the subdivision. Up to three teams of 7 play the mall and the subdivision. Choose the map on the Home screen; the official server plays them in turn. Pick a primary and a secondary gun and fit them with sights, magazines and more; everyone starts with armour and a helmet. Hosts set how well the bots play: Easy, Normal, Hard or Pro. Team Wipe is the default: the last team standing earns 1 point, in a race to 10, 20, 30 or 50. Everyone returns with full health and their chosen guns for each new round. The mall's and the yard's fields are disabled by default; hosts can enable the usual countdown and closing schedule, capped at half their starting radius. Kill-score, timed-score and single-round elimination rules are also available.

The first player in the lobby is its host: they choose the settings and press Start, once everyone else is Ready (alone, at once). Bots fill the empty seats. Join during a match to watch it, then play the next one.

Get the game

Your game must be the same version as the servers: 0.25.0.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

  1. Download AetherionLauncher.exe and open it.
  2. If Windows says Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. The launcher is not code-signed yet.
  3. The launcher installs itself for your user (no admin needed) and adds a Start menu and desktop shortcut. Press Install to download the game: 1.25 GB download, 2.06 GB on disk.
  4. Press Play. Later updates download only what changed.
Download the launcher978 KB · version 1.4.0

Already installed? Open Aetherion Project from the Start menu. If it shows Update, press it, then Play.

Crashing on a laptop with Intel graphics? In the game open Settings > Graphics > Renderer and choose Safe graphics, then restart. The game also switches by itself after a crash on such laptops. Updating the Intel graphics driver helps too.

Android

Android 7.0 or newer, 64-bit · about 1.2 GB free space

  1. On your phone, download the APK. It is 690 MB, so use Wi-Fi.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  4. For a new version, download the APK again and install it over the old one. Your profile stays.
Download the APK690 MB · version 0.25.0

Black screen or a crash when the game starts? Install the compatibility build (690 MB) instead. It draws with OpenGL, which older phones handle better.

Mac, iPhone and Linux are not in this playtest yet. Play on a Windows PC or an Android phone for now.

How to playtest

Play together: agree on a time with the other testers so you land in the same match.

  1. 1

    Set your name

    Open Profile in the main menu and type the name other players will see.

  2. 2

    Choose a mode

    On Home, pick Classic (battle royale) or Deathmatch.

  3. 3

    Tap Play online

    Under Official server · Singapore, tap Play online. You join that mode's server in one tap.

  4. 4

    Start or ready up

    If you are the first one in, you are the host: pick the map and settings, then press Start match. Otherwise tap Ready; the host starts once everyone is ready. If a match is already running, you watch it and play the next one.

Play now on the Home screen is offline practice against bots. Use it to learn the controls while you wait for other testers.

Update · 0.25.0

Smoother firefights in the mall

Big fights in Malasugue Mall run more smoothly, and your gun behaves the way you expect at a sprint.

  • Shattered glass no longer piles up for the rest of the match, and gunfire, impacts and dust cost far less when many players shoot at once.
  • Your gun stays in your hands while you sprint, carried low across your chest.
  • Pull the trigger at a sprint and you slow to a run straight away. Let go and the sprint picks up again.
  • Less muzzle smoke: no more glowing cloud in front of your sights in automatic fire.
  • Running up the mall's fire stairs at an angle no longer launches you up them.

Update · 0.24.0

Build your gun on the Workbench

Press K in a match, or tap Gear on a phone, and your gun turns on a stand in front of you.

  • Drag an attachment onto the gun and it snaps into place. Hover one to see exactly what it changes before you fit it.
  • 11 new attachments, among them drum magazines that double your rounds, a marksman suppressor, a green laser and a quick-deploy sling.
  • Scopes show a clear picture: thermal marks enemies with warm brackets, night vision brightens the dark in natural colour.
  • Hold ~ for a full scoreboard, and see who won each Deathmatch round with a 3-second countdown to the next.
  • Bots follow waypoints on every map, and can finally cross the river in Sitio Kalinaw.

Deathmatch update · 0.22.0

Bots that come and find you

Bots no longer camp in one spot until the round stalls, and the host now chooses how well they play.

  • Pick the bot difficulty: Easy, Normal, Hard or Pro. Pro bots fight by their gun: scoped at range with a sniper rifle, rushing you with a pistol, pushing when you reload.
  • When a side is down to its last few, bots sweep the whole map with their team until they find you.
  • Fit sights, muzzles, grips, magazines, stocks and lights to both guns in Game Setup. Each gun remembers its setup.
  • Big firefights no longer drag the frame rate down, on Freight Delight above all.
  • A thrown grenade comes with a radio-distorted "Fire in the hole!", and Freight Delight has 40 more lights.

Map · 0.21.0

Freight Delight

A squatter settlement has grown inside an old container yard by the port. It is always night, and two teams of up to 12 fight through it.

  • Cross by the Container Canyon, through the Bodega and the crane plaza, or along the Eskinita shanty alleys.
  • Take the second floor: container decks, plank bridges over the plaza, the crane platform and the Taas windows. Three stairs lead up.
  • The lamps carry the light: floodlights, bare bulbs on wires, flickering tubes, videoke neon and shrine candles, with 40 more in the darkest lanes since 0.22.0.

What's new in 0.25.0

  • Less lag in big firefights, most of all in Malasugue Mall. Shattered glass no longer piles up for the rest of the match (a match's worth of broken shopfronts used to leave thousands of pieces behind), and gunfire, bullet impacts and dust cost your computer much less when many players shoot at once.
  • Your gun stays in your hands while you sprint, carried low across your chest, instead of disappearing.
  • Shooting while sprinting slows you to a run straight away. Let go of the trigger and the sprint picks up again.
  • Less muzzle smoke. Your own gun no longer puts a glowing cloud in front of your sights during automatic fire, and other guns leave only a faint wisp.
  • Malasugue Mall: running up the fire stairs at an angle no longer launches you up them at nearly twice your speed.
  • Update the game to 0.25.0 on every device to join the updated servers.
